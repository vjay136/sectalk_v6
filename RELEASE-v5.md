# SecTalk 5.0.0

- **Inbox and calls**. Message bubbles include names, timestamps, read receipts, and menus with replies, pins, edits, reports, download, Delete for me, and Revoke for everyone. Call panels offer conversation and reporting shortcuts for direct calls. Group meetings offer a media disconnect option.
- **Emoji**. The message composer and each message's reaction picker include 3,395 native emoji choices and 10,816 SecTalk combinations. The combinations pair a recognizable emoji with a badge. The server validates reactions against the catalog. Each participant can add up to eight or remove their own.
- **Attachments**. Accepted contacts can send images, PDFs, and text files of up to 4 MiB. The app delivers downloads without inline previews. Revoking a message stops new app downloads. Previously downloaded copies and explicitly shared moderation evidence can remain. Managed malware and nudity scanning are pending.
- **Find Friends**. New accounts can choose their interests during onboarding. Choose up to eight categories from 1,108 occupation, study, and hobby choices. The Inbox has a Find Friends shortcut and separate Incoming and Sent request views. Category discovery requires opt-in and an 18+ self-attestation. Exact handle requests remain available, and calls and messaging require acceptance.
- **Profile**. Manage a photo and 140-character text status, check visible-message and report counts, open report timelines, control last seen, and block or unblock contacts across direct chat, audio, and video. Photos and text status are visible to accepted contacts when neither side blocks the other.
- **Safety**. Local warning rules flag patterns associated with scams, spam, harassment, threats, and intimate requests. Users can report unwanted sexual content and choose whether to share an attached file. Staff with MFA can warn, escalate, restrict for 24 hours or seven days, restrict indefinitely, or restore access. An indefinite restriction prevents new communication while preserving access to account notices. Reporters can track case stages and actions without seeing private staff notes. Rules provide advisory signals and staff make moderation decisions.

The v5 deployment uses the shared Supabase project. Revokes and staff restrictions are enforced in the backend and apply across connected releases. Delete for me is reflected in the v5 UI. The older builds and their URLs stay available.

The next release still needs the hosted Supabase email redirect and verified SMTP configuration. Group media still needs LiveKit, and paid checkout still needs Stripe. The [master specification](MASTER-PROMPT.md) also tracks end-to-end encrypted messaging, malware and media scanning, enterprise identity, and other future work.
