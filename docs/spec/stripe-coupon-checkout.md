# Managed Checkout coupons

Enter one code in **Billing → Compare plans** before opening Stripe Checkout. SecTalk accepts one managed code per purchase, applies one Stripe discount and disables additional promotion entry at Stripe. Ordinary Checkout remains available without a code. Coupon purchases use card Checkout, so paid and expired provider transitions can be settled reliably without relying on delayed bank payments.

Assign a designated staff member in the private coupon operator table. With fresh TOTP, that operator can call the authenticated `st_v7_publish_coupon` RPC with the uppercase campaign code, expected current revision, and version terms. Terms include active dates, eligible plans and currencies, a total redemption limit, a Stripe coupon ID, and either percentage in basis points or a fixed amount in the eligible currency's minor units. Set the duration to once, repeating for 1–36 months, or forever. Publish a disabled revision to pause new reservations. Provider coupon duration, amount, cap, environment, and applicable product are verified before the app creates a Checkout session.

SecTalk reserves capacity before opening Checkout. A reservation counts against the campaign cap until Stripe confirms expiry, or until Stripe confirms the scoped matching discount, line item, account and paid or no-payment-required completion. Replays cannot reopen terminal rows. Reservations and redemptions are counted across campaign revisions. A user can redeem a given campaign once.

Subscribe the signed Stripe webhook to Checkout Session completed and expired events. As a fallback, the private reconciliation worker checks bound sessions after their expiry. An ambiguous provider failure before the app learns the session ID retains a hold until matching signed evidence arrives or an operator independently verifies the provider outcome. This conservative hold avoids reissuing accepted capacity during a provider outage. Provision and verify the matching live Stripe coupons separately.
