# Stripe invoice documents

Open **Billing → Invoices and receipts** to view finalized invoices for your linked subscription. An active delegated billing administrator can open the same page from the workspace billing dashboard. SecTalk checks the Stripe customer and subscription on the request and on every returned invoice. Download permissions are checked again after rendering, so revocation also rejects previously issued tickets.

Stripe assigns sequential invoice numbers during finalization. SecTalk pins each finalized invoice to its provider number, issuance time, customer, and subscription in a private registry. Test and live numbering have separate namespaces. The registry detects conflicting assignments and preserves leading zeroes. SecTalk neither generates new provider invoice numbers nor renumbers drafts, voids, or revisions. Stripe owns the issuer’s configured numbering policy.

Downloads are SecTalk PDF copies of finalized Stripe invoice data. The invoice lists original currency, totals, balances and reported taxes. Each tax line retains its amount, taxable amount when available, and inclusive or exclusive treatment. Amounts in other currencies are retained as exact provider minor units. A receipt copy is available when Stripe reports the invoice paid and provides a payment date, including amounts settled outside Stripe or through credits. The app never includes card fields, customer contact data, or hosted Stripe links in these copies. Official tax invoice PDFs and processor-specific receipts remain in the owner’s Stripe self-service flow.

Set `SECTALK_STRIPE_DOCUMENT_SECRET` in the server secret store to a separate random value at least 32 bytes long. This signs actor-, workspace-, customer- and subscription-bound tickets that last three minutes. Keep the same secret across deployed instances. Requests perform read-only Stripe operations. Local tests use synthetic finalized records and generate actual PDFs. Hosted Stripe numbering and document availability require deployment verification.
