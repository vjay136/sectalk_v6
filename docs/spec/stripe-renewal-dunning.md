# Stripe renewals and dunning

Subscribe the signed billing webhook to Stripe automatic renewal invoice payment failures, paid and payment succeeded events, invoice updates, voids and uncollectible changes. Keep customer subscription lifecycle events enabled. SecTalk reads the current provider invoice and subscription, validates the linked customer and subscription, then updates its durable receipt and billing state together. Configuration of actual payment retries remains in Stripe Billing. SecTalk never initiates a charge or creates a payment attempt.

**Billing** shows the grace deadline and the next attempt when Stripe has scheduled one. The deployment policy in the private `sectalk_private.st_v7_renewal_policy` row defaults to 72 hours and accepts 24–336 hours. The earliest confirmed failure fixes each installment's deadline. Later failures can clarify the first failed time but cannot extend grace. Past-due entitlement uses the earliest still-open renewal deadline. Payment, invoice closure or terminal subscription status clears the corresponding notices. When a past-due subscription has no known open installment, grace falls back to the end of the provider period plus the configured policy. Trial timing and degraded billing reads are separate.

Schedule the existing private Stripe reconciliation endpoint to recover missed updates for linked subscriptions and known open installments. The worker probes at most six due invoices per pass, with ten minutes between attempts. A missing provider record or provider outage leaves account state intact.

Schedule `POST /api/billing/renewal-mail` with a dedicated `SECTALK_RENEWAL_MAIL_SECRET` bearer of at least 32 bytes and the existing notification SMTP configuration. Transactional renewal messages use the account's currently confirmed email. They contain the app billing link and timing, and omit provider payment details. The queue deduplicates provider attempts, supersedes unsent earlier attempts and checks live eligibility immediately before sending. SMTP transport retries are bounded separately from payment retries. A crash after SMTP acceptance can cause a repeated submission with the same Message-ID. Configure hosted Stripe retry rules and both private scheduler calls during deployment.
